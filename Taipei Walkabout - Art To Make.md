# Taipei Walkabout: art to make

This is every picture the game still needs, plus a few existing ones worth redoing. It replaces the older image list. It is ordered by how much each picture improves the game right now. 小雨's story comes first, because it is the route we are building properly.

> **Status (21 Oct 2026):** 93 pictures are now in the game: all of Parts 1 to 6, the first 13 book covers, and 阿凯's new happy picture. **Still to make:** the 12 newer book covers in Part 7 (`book_guide`, `book_lost`, `book_awei`, `book_case1` to `book_case5`, `book_chabuduo`, `book_luohuasheng`, `book_beiying`, `book_kongyiji`). Everything else up to Part 7 is done. **New:** Part 8 has four optional pictures for the MRT ride.

**What the game has today:** 30 character pictures (10 people), 5 food pictures and 1 background. Everything else on this list shows a placeholder, a blurred street photo or a plain coloured card until you make it.

**How to use this**
1. In ChatGPT, paste one prompt at a time, followed by the style line for that section.
2. Rename the download to the file name shown, exactly as written in lower case. ChatGPT gives you PNG files, so you can keep `.png` even where the name says `.jpg`. I'll convert them when you send them.
3. Attach the pictures in this chat and I'll add them to the game and update the website.

**Sizes:** portrait 1024 × 1536 for people, landscape 1536 × 1024 for everything else.

**Keeping faces the same:** for anyone who is already in the game (小雨, 美美), first upload their existing picture to ChatGPT and say *"This is the character. Keep her face, hair and proportions exactly the same."* I've sent you 小雨's and 美美's current pictures to use as references. For new people, make the `_neutral` picture first. Then, in the **same chat**, ask for the other expressions with *"Same character, same outfit, same style. Now make the … version."*

## Existing pictures: what needs improving

I looked through all 36 pictures that are in the game now. They are consistent and good quality, so most need no work. Four things are worth knowing:

1. **小雨 holds a bubble tea in all three of her pictures.** That's fine at the shop, but in sad and romantic moments she is still holding a drink. Parts 1a and 1b fix this, so there is no need to redo the old ones.
2. **阿凯's neutral and happy pictures look almost the same** (same pose, similar face). This is optional. To redo `char_akai_happy.png`, upload his neutral picture and ask: *"Same character, same outfit. Now make him laughing, one hand raised in a friendly wave, the menu folder tucked under his other arm."*
3. **The game has only one background** (`bg_mrt_train.jpg`, the train). Every shop conversation sits on a blurred street photo, which is why Part 2 matters so much.
4. **The character files are large** (about 1.3 MB each), which slows loading on phones. You don't need to do anything about this. I'll shrink them without visible loss next time I update the game.
